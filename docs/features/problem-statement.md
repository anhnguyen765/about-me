# Project: Anh Nguyen — text-first portfolio

## Problem

Recruiters, collaborators, and peers need a memorable, fast way to understand Anh Nguyen's product perspective and explore selected work. The current site contains the right material, but presents every page with similar weight and does not make a strong personal editorial impression.

## What I'm building

A creative, editorial personal portfolio for Anh Nguyen that balances work and life. A poetic product philosophy, typography, and narrative structure carry the experience; original cool-blue blurred-gradient components (`#17CAFA` and `#0E56FA`) and selected images provide energy without overpowering the content.

## Must work (v1)

- [ ] A distinctive gradient-led homepage positions Anh as an interesting human with sharp product thinking and potential guest-speaker presence within the first viewport; the portrait appears later.
- [ ] Visitors can navigate readable, recruiter-friendly pages for work, projects, thinking/writing, biography, and personal moments.
- [ ] A shared text-led visual system uses oversized editorial typography, purposeful spacing, blurred-gradient components, and responsive layouts across every route.
- [ ] Existing images and work samples remain accessible but never overpower the written narrative.
- [ ] Navigation, content, links, and contact details are accessible and work well from a 320px viewport upward.

## Not doing yet

- A CMS or authoring workflow
- New case-study content beyond the existing portfolio material
- Motion-heavy interactions or decorative visual effects that distract from the story
- A full archive of every historical project or photograph

## Done means

The portfolio feels unmistakably personal and culturally current, lets a hiring manager grasp Anh's product story and standout projects in under a minute, and makes inviting Anh to speak or collaborate feel natural.
